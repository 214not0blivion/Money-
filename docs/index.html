How it works

Five steps from request to finished kitchen

Typical turnaround is two to three weeks from template to installation.

1

Submit your request

Fill out the booking form with your measurements, material, and the dates that work for you. It takes about five minutes and gives us everything we need to quote.

2

Consultation & slab selection

We call within one business day to confirm details and set up a slab viewing if your material is a natural stone.

3

On-site template

Cabinets must be installed and level. We laser-measure, confirm your sink and cooktop, mark seam locations, and collect the deposit. Final price is locked in here.

4

Fabrication

Seven to ten business days in the shop: cutting, edge work, cutouts, and polishing. We send a layout photo before we cut so you can approve the veining.

5

Installation day

Old tops out, new tops in, seams set and sealed. Plan on four to eight hours for a typical kitchen. Plumbing reconnects the next day after the adhesive cures.

Materials

Pick the surface that fits how you actually cook

Every material trades something. Here's the short version — the full comparison covers durability, sealing, and price.

Granite

Natural, heat-resistant, and every slab is one of a kind. Needs sealing about once a year.

Quartz

Engineered and non-porous. No sealing, consistent color, the lowest-maintenance option.

Marble

Unmatched for looks and pastry work. Etches and stains — it earns a patina rather than staying pristine.

Quartzite

Natural stone with marble's look and granite's toughness. Premium price, premium wear.
