Before you book

The five things we'll ask you for

Have these ready and the booking form takes about five minutes.

1

Job site address and access notes

Where the work happens, and anything that makes delivery hard — stairs, a narrow driveway, an elevator, a gate code, a third-floor walkup.

2

Approximate square footage

Length times depth for each run, in feet, added together. Here's how to measure it yourself. A rough number is fine — we confirm it at template.

3

Material and color, if you've decided

If you haven't, say so on the form. We'll bring options to the consultation rather than making you guess.

4

Your sink and cooktop

How many sinks, undermount or drop-in, how many faucet holes, and whether a cooktop drops into the counter. Cutouts are priced individually.

5

Your dates

When we can template, and when you need to be finished. If you're working around a contractor or a holiday, tell us on the form.
